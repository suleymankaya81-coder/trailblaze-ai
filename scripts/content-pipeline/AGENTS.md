- Tier 1 wave preparation saves reusable non-AI photo, coordinate and route facts before paid AI generation; publish only after the existing completeness checks. Why: a credit limit must not waste repeated fact collection or expose incomplete places.
- New waves use run-content-wave.py with combined summary/permit/plan generation, reviewed compact exact excerpts and source-bound stop IDs; retain failed attempts without automatic replay. Why: avoid repeated paid context while preserving evidence and publishing gates.
- England wave-2 sources are tracked in uk/england-sources.json; collected packets stay unapproved until exact excerpts and stop identities are reviewed, and combined summaries validate permit/season enums before writes. Why: fetching a URL is not evidence approval and free-text enum values cannot be persisted.
- Combined batches save validated retained plans through save_reviewed_bundle with draft-only guarded readback, and require explicit apply confirmation. Why: skipped legacy writers must not leave corrected local output absent from the database or falsely report success.
- New search-guide pages must be at least 600 words (generator rewrites once, insert marks shorter pages noindex); earlier pages keep their original review. Why: thin pages rank poorly.
- Bulk guide generation (generate-landing-pages.py) uses google/gemini-3-flash-preview via streamed chat completions. Why: user chose it for lower cost with the same 600+ word checks.
- Search titles are trimmed to ~60 chars and meta descriptions to 160 by insert-landing-pages.py; destination meta descriptions are composed from tagline, best season and guide topics. Why: Google truncates longer snippets.
- Travel content is generated in bounded sequential Gemini batches with cached outputs, database lease and pause status; validation rejects incomplete days/local notes and changing rules link to official sources. Why: reruns preserve work and credit/policy blocks stop the whole pipeline.
- Marker generation applies the dated travel review ledger through travel_review_rules.py, withholding wrong identities and unverified parent-feature points. Why: title matching and a regional bounding box cannot establish a sightseeing location.
- Catalog destinations go live in waves via `scripts/content-pipeline/{prepare-wave,apply-wave}.py` → `generate/insert-landing-pages.py` → `publish-wave.py`; a place is published only when every check passes (cover, location, offers, route, reservation note, local plan, card photos); search guides are optional and not generated for new places. Why: same destination quality without spending credits on guides the owner does not want.
- Affiliate feeds: raw files in data/affiliate-feeds/<brand>/raw, normalized by import-<brand>-feed.py into products.json. Why: one re-importable catalog for product guides.
- collect-route-stops.py stores canonical daily routing identities and a missing-coordinate report; only exact reviewed source-marker matches receive coordinates. Why: translated labels and broad park-centre matches must not change navigation.
- Canada places come from scripts/content-pipeline/canada/catalog.json (wave, authority, road/fly-in); CA regions stay draft until a place in them is published. Why: one reviewed source and no empty province pages.
- Canadian corrections and rejected photo selections live in canada/review.json and override generation retries. Why: verified corrections must not revert to the original generated mistakes.
- All future content writers require an approved source-packets/<slug>.json through source_evidence.py; official evidence governs operational claims and absent evidence holds generation. Why: source links and model recollection alone cannot establish facts.
- Offline bulk audits reuse fetched source documents, record per-claim evidence gaps and apply only textual corrections with exact supplied quotes plus guarded database readback. Why: avoid repeated agent research and false completeness claims.
- UK places come from scripts/content-pipeline/uk/catalog.json (country gb, regions england/scotland/wales/northern-ireland, duplicates merged, 15 national parks as pilot wave); GB regions stay draft until a place is published and authority /eu/uk pages map to /destinations/gb only after UK_PAGES_LIVE. Why: one reviewed source, no empty pages.
- Travel-plan generation holds any destination whose daily stop names do not appear in its approved source packet, or whose citations are not packet URLs; held plans are cleared so a rerun regenerates them. Why: sourced pages must not contain model-recalled places.
- Travel generation strips link targets from evidence text before prompting and matches stop names by their distinctive words (generic words like trail/centre ignored). Why: models cited linked pages never fetched, and exact-string matching rejected sourced places.
