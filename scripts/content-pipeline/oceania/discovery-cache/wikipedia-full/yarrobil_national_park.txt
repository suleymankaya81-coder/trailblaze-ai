Yarrobil National Park is located in New South Wales, Australia. It is located 21 kilometres (13 mi) north west of Gulgong.
The park covers 1,322 hectares (3,270 acres) in three disconnected sections.  It was previously a State forest and was converted to a national park in December 2005.
It is located at an altitude of 546 meters.
