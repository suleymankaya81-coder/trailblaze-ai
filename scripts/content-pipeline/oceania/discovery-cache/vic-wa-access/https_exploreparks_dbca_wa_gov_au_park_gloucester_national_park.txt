About this park
Gloucester National Park has lots of walking trails and you can mountain bike here too. Pick a trail and take a journey through the beautiful karri forest of the park. Karri trees only grow in southwestern Australia. They’re the tallest type of tree in the State and can grow up to 95m high. You can recognise them by their tall straight trunk and smooth bark.
In Spring and Summer, the karri canopy bursts into white flowers. The rowdy calls overhead will be from flocks of purple-crowned lorikeets. They flock in to feast on the flowers. Birdwatchers may be lucky to spot honeyeaters, wrens, fantails and robins. Sit quietly and keep your eyes peeled on the undergrowth.
This park is also home to Western Australia’s most famous climbing karri tree, the Gloucester Tree. Come for a climb, a picnic or explore one of the many trails.
The forest is home to many small native animals. These include quenda, quokkas, mardos and dunnarts. It’s unlikely you’ll see these shy and elusive creatures. But it’s good to know they’re here.

Trails
Gloucester Route
Bush Walk
10 kilometres
This 10km moderate trail loops through towering karri forest; over hills; down gullies and across hidden creeks and waterfalls. A rewarding and beautiful trail commencing in the Gloucester National Park near Pemberton.
View this trail
Gloucester Tree to Cascades Walk
Bush Walk
12 kilometres
This 12km return walk starts from the world's tallest climbable tree - the Gloucester Tree and finishes at the Cascades, a series of babbling rapids along Lefroy Brook. It uses the Bibbulmun Track.
View this trail
Gloucester Tree Walk
Bush Walk
6 kilometres
This 6km return walk takes you from Pemberton town site to the Gloucester Tree or visa versa. The Gloucester tree is the world's tallest climbable tree - and the view from the top is amazing!
View this trail
Karri Views
Bush Walk
800 metres
This path leads you to the edge of the valley. It offers views across the bubbling Eastbrook below. It offers views across the Gloucester National Park.
View this trail
Lefroy Brook Loop Walk
Bush Walk
1.2 kilometres
Set amid the karri forest about 8km south of Pemberton, these rocky rapids and surrounds provide a place for an outdoor lunch, a leisurely afternoon stroll, or a few peaceful hours of fly fishing.
View this trail
The Dukes Walk
Bush Walk
400 metres
Follow the blue markers and catch your first close-up glimpse of the karri forest from this short loop trail. Only 3km from Pemberton, this trail starts from the famous Gloucester Tree.
