About this park
Korung National Park is less than an hour’s drive from Perth. It’s a great place to bush walk and mountain bike but there are no visitor facilities.  A network of walk trails starts outside the park and criss-cross through the park. Which one will you choose? Mountain bikers can explore on two wheels on the
Munda Biddi Trail
that runs through parts of the park.
Escape the burbs into a wonderland of wildflowers in springtime. Whatever the season, you will enjoy its natural appeal.
Review
- Great walking trails
I have walked many trails in this great National Park, the Lion Lookout trail is quite short but very challenging due to very steep descents, Victoria Dam, Bickley Reservoir, Munday Brook trail and many more. The abundance of wildflowers in Spring is amazing. Once you get used to the signage it’s quite easy to follow the trails without getting lost. A must visit for everyone trying to escape the Perth suburbia.
Jana C - Trip Advisor

Trails
Lions Lookout Walk
Bush Walk
4 kilometres
The Lions Lookout Walk Trail is a four kilometre loop trail providing expansive views over the Perth Coastal Plain and an abundance of wildflowers in spring. Perfect for a short walk through the bush without having to travel too far.
View this trail
Munday Brook Walk
Bush Walk
9.9 kilometres
A long circuit walk through mixed forest of the Korung National Park, located in picturesque Canning Mills in the Perth Hills. A mostly flat trail with some slightly steeper sections, Munday Brook Walk is an enjoyable 9.9km walk through native bushland.
