About this park
This park lies on the slopes of Greenmount Hill overlooking Perth. It’s one of the smaller national parks along the Darling Scarp. The natural landscape is an attractive setting for recreation and family outings. This area has significance in Aboriginal folklore and was mentioned in early European settler’s diaries. Take a bushwalk and enjoy the tranquility of the bush. There’s lots of wildlife around so keep your eyes peeled. Greenmount Lookout offers splendid views of the Swan Coastal Plain and the City of Perth.
Unpack your ropes and harnesses and get a foothold at
Mountain Quarry
This blue stone quarry is a popular hangout for rock climbers and walkers.
If mountain biking’s more your thing, then head over to
The Goat Farm
. This mountain bike park lets you practice and develop your cross-country mountain-bike skills. It may be small, but this National Park is up there for adventure sports activities, as well as walking and nature watching.

Mountain Bike

The Goat Farm Mountain Bike Park is a totally designated mtb area. On the outskirts of Perth, only 4km from Midland station, it is a popular first stop for Perth riders. XC, DH and 4X riders are catered for. The stand out is the new skills park.
