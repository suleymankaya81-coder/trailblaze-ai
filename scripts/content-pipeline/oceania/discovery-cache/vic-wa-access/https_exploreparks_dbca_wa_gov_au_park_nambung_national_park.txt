About this park
All within a couple of hours drive of Perth this park has many highlights. The Pinnacles Desert is surrounded by coastal plains, white sand dunes, and beautiful swimming beaches.
The shifting dune systems and low heathland support a rich variety of flowering plants. This vegetation bursts into flower from August to October, creating a memorable spectacle. Blooming wildflowers and vistas of wattles stretch from horizon to horizon.
Walk the loop trail and boardwalk at
Lake Thetis
and step back in time. Find out about stromatolites, no not meteorites! Stromatolites are rock-like structures that date back to the dawn of life on earth. Wow!
Come back down to earth with a swim at one of the park’s beaches. Offshore is a stunning reef system protected in the
Jurien Bay Marine Park
.
Nambung National Park has all year-round interest and makes a great day trip from Perth.
Review
- Otherwordly experience
Nambung National Park is a fantastic, otherworldly experience! There is plenty to occupy your time here: The Desert Discovery Centre (Visitor Centre) is very well done; the 4km desert drive is fun and the 1.2km desert walk is a surreal experience! The geology here is very unique and definitely not to be missed!
Keith-B-Robinson – Trip Advisor

Attractions
Pinnacles Desert Lookout and Drive
Thousands of huge limestone pillars rise from a stark landscape of yellow sand to form one of Australia’s most intriguing landscapes.
Read more
Pinnacles Desert Discovery
Stop in at the discovery centre to see displays, soundscapes, videos and objects that explain the geology of the pinnacles' formations and the cultural and natural heritage values of the area.
Read more
Lake Thetis
Fascinating structures called stromatolites at Lake Thetis provide insights into what life was like at the dawn of time.
Read more
Kangaroo Point
An idyllic beach popular for shore fishing and boating.
Read more
Hangover Bay
With its wide sweep of sandy beach, Hangover Bay offers good snorkelling, swimming, windsurfing and surfing in the Jurien Bay Marine Park .

Grey
Grey is a great beach area for swimming and fishing on the Nambung National Park coast, south of the Pinnacles.

Desert View Walk Trail
Bush Walk
1.5 kilometres
The trail begins on a 300m, Class 1 accessible path to a viewing platform, before continuing as a Class 3 sandy trail for 1.2km winding amongst the spectacular limestone pinnacles.
