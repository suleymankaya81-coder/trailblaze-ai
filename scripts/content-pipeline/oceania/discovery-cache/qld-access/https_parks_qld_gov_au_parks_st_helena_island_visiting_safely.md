## Getting there and getting around

- [St Helena Island National Park map(PDF, 1.7MB)](https://parks.qld.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0015/160440/st_helena_map.pdf)

St Helena Island is 7km north-east of Manly, a suburb of Brisbane, and 45 minutes by boat. The Moreton Bay Environmental Education Centre operates its own service to the island for school groups. Book through the centre—contact details are listed under [tourism information links](https://parks.qld.gov.au/parks/st-helena-island/about#tourism_information_links).

Private boats may be moored off the island. Consider the tides when anchoring in shallow water.

The southern corner of the island is open to the public without a tour guide. The rest of the island, including the prison ruins, is a restricted access area and entry is by [guided tour](https://parks.qld.gov.au/parks/st-helena-island/about#tourism_information_links) only.

### Wheelchair accessibility

There are wheelchair-accessible toilets on the island. The grassy paths and crushed coral roads around the island are not easily accessible for strollers or wheelchairs. Contact the [park office](https://parks.qld.gov.au/parks/st-helena-island/about#further_information) for further information about accessibility.

## Staying safe

For your safety and to protect the ruins, please stay behind the rope barriers. Please read the guidelines on [safety in parks and forests](https://parks.qld.gov.au/before-you-visit/visit-with-care).

## Before you visit

### Essentials to bring

It is recommended that visitors bring a hat, sunscreen, walking shoes, insect repellent and drinking water.

### Opening hours

The national park is open during daylight hours. Camping and fishing are not permitted.

### Permits and fees

According to tour operators.

### Pets

Domestic animals are not permitted in St Helena Island National Park.

### Climate and weather

The Brisbane region enjoys a mild, subtropical climate. The average daily temperature range is 22 to 30 degrees Celsius in summer and 12 to 22 degrees Celsius in winter.

### Fuel and supplies

There are no supplies on the island. Fuel and supplies are available in Brisbane.

For more information see the [tourism information links](https://parks.qld.gov.au/parks/st-helena-island/about#tourism_information_links).

## Our booking system is currently unavailable

We have temporarily deactivated our Queensland National Parks booking system following a cyber security incident involving our third-party booking provider, Bookeasy.

**Queensland's national parks, state forests and recreation areas remain open.**

While the booking system is unavailable:

- **Camping:** You can camp without a camping permit.
- **Vehicle access:** You can access K'gari, Cooloola and Bribie Island recreation areas without a vehicle access permit.
- **Visitor centres:** David Fleay Wildlife Park, Mon Repos Turtle Centre and Walkabout Creek Discovery Centre remain open.
- **Tours and experiences:** Existing bookings, including Mon Repos Turtle Tours, will go ahead. New bookings can be made on the day of the tour, in person, pending capacity.
- **Existing bookings:** All existing bookings and permits remain valid, however we cannot make changes or cancellations.
- **Permits**: Visitors will not be penalised for not holding a camping or vehicle access permit while the temporary arrangements are in place.
- **Future bookings:** Please refer to our website for any updates.

Check [Park Alerts](https://parks.qld.gov.au/park-alerts) for the open/closed status of individual parks before travelling.

For booking enquiries, please email [QPWS@detsi.qld.gov.au](mailto:QPWS@detsi.qld.gov.au).

**All other normal rules apply when visiting our National Parks.**
