## Getting there and getting around

Holbourne Island National Park is the most northerly national park island in the Whitsundays. It is remote and has no facilities. Access to the park is by private boat only.

Getting to the park can present navigational challenges. Always take the weather and tidal influences into account when boating in the Whitsundays. Ensure you read [Planning your trip to the Whitsundays](https://parks.qld.gov.au/parks/parks-whitsundays/visiting-safely#planning_your_trip) and [Getting there and getting around the Whitsundays](https://parks.qld.gov.au/parks/parks-whitsundays/visiting-safely#getting_there_and_getting_around) before your departure.

Visitors must be self-sufficient and should have a marine radio. This continental island is completely surrounded by a Marine Conservation Park (yellow) zone and restrictions on some activities apply. Obtain your zoning map from the [Great Barrier Reef Marine Park Authority(external link)](http://www.gbrmpa.gov.au/) or a local bait and tackle shop. Ensure you consult your map.

Although the weather is sunny and tropical most of the year, conditions can change quickly. Cyclones can also pose a hazard to campers isolated on islands. [Stay safe](https://parks.qld.gov.au/parks/parks-whitsundays/visiting-safely#staying_safe) and in touch to enjoy your visit. For up-to-date weather information, check the [Bureau of Meteorology(external link)](http://www.bom.gov.au/) website.

### Wheelchair accessibility

There are no wheelchair-accessible facilities on Holbourne Island National Park.

## Staying safe

Holbourne Island National Park is isolated and remote. There is limited recreational use of the island and visitors need to be well prepared and self-sufficient.

To enjoy a safe visit, read more about [staying safe](https://parks.qld.gov.au/parks/parks-whitsundays/visiting-safely#staying_safe) in national parks of the Whitsundays.

## Before you visit

Our precious Great Barrier Reef World Heritage islands are among the most pest-free islands in the world. They need your help to stay this way. Please [Be pest-free!(PDF, 573KB)](https://parks.qld.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0028/155557/be-pest-free-gbr-broch.pdf) before your visit.

Please also read [things to know before you go](https://parks.qld.gov.au/parks/parks-whitsundays/visiting-safely#before_you_visit) to national parks of the Whitsundays.

## Our booking system is currently unavailable

We have temporarily deactivated our Queensland National Parks booking system following a cyber security incident involving our third-party booking provider, Bookeasy.

**Queensland's national parks, state forests and recreation areas remain open.**

While the booking system is unavailable:

- **Camping:** You can camp without a camping permit.
- **Vehicle access:** You can access K'gari, Cooloola and Bribie Island recreation areas without a vehicle access permit.
- **Visitor centres:** David Fleay Wildlife Park, Mon Repos Turtle Centre and Walkabout Creek Discovery Centre remain open.
- **Tours and experiences:** Existing bookings, including Mon Repos Turtle Tours, will go ahead. New bookings can be made on the day of the tour, in person, pending capacity.
- **Existing bookings:** All existing bookings and permits remain valid, however we cannot make changes or cancellations.
- **Permits**: Visitors will not be penalised for not holding a camping or vehicle access permit while the temporary arrangements are in place.
- **Future bookings:** Please refer to our website for any updates.

Check [Park Alerts](https://parks.qld.gov.au/park-alerts) for the open/closed status of individual parks before travelling.

For booking enquiries, please email [QPWS@detsi.qld.gov.au](mailto:QPWS@detsi.qld.gov.au).

**All other normal rules apply when visiting our National Parks.**
