## Getting there and getting around

- [Fort Lytton locality map(PDF, 995KB)](https://parks.qld.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0023/164309/fort-lytton-locality-map.pdf)

**From central Brisbane:** travel east along Lytton Road. Drive through the Hemmant Industrial area, then swing left to South Street and through the gate to Fort Lytton.

**From Wynnum:** drive west to the end of Tingal Road, left into Pritchard Street towards Brisbane and right into Lytton  Road to the park entrance.

- For more information about when to visit, please see [opening hours](https://parks.qld.gov.au/parks/fort-lytton/visiting-safely#opening_hours).

### Wheelchair accessibility

There are wheelchair-accessible toilets. The grass pathways and fortifications at Fort  Lytton are not easily accessible for wheelchairs or strollers. [Contact us](https://parks.qld.gov.au/parks/fort-lytton/about#further_information) for further information about accessibility.

## Staying safe

For information, please read the guidelines on [safety in parks and forests](https://parks.qld.gov.au/before-you-visit/visit-with-care).

## Before you visit

### Essentials to bring

Wear a hat and sunscreen while walking around the site.

If you plan to have a barbecue, bring a portable gas barbecue, as open fires are not permitted in the park.

### Opening hours

- Fort Lytton is open to the public every Sunday and most public holidays from 10am to 4pm. The park is closed outside these specified opening hours.
- Entry is free and bookings are not necessary unless a specific time slot for a tour is required. The first tour is at 10.30am and the last tour is at 1.45pm. Tours take approximately 90 minutes.
- Group bookings for other times can be arranged by appointment and [fees apply](https://parks.qld.gov.au/parks/fort-lytton/visiting-safely#permits_and_fees).
- To make a tour booking, please [contact us](https://parks.qld.gov.au/parks/fort-lytton/about#further_information).
- For more information about the guided tours see [things to do](https://parks.qld.gov.au/parks/fort-lytton/things-to-do).

### Permits and fees

- **Sundays:** free entry, no bookings necessary unless a specific time slot is required for a tour.
- **Weekdays:** Adult $7.60, child and concession $4.15 (tertiary students, pensioners, concession card holders)\* (Note: bookings are essential for weekday visits).

\\* Concessional entry requires a valid Australian concession card to be presented at the time of purchase/booking. Holders of a valid Companion Card receive free entry (one companion only) when accompanying a valid Concession Card holder who has purchased an admission ticket.

### Pets

Domestic animals are not permitted in Fort Lytton National Park.

### Climate and weather

Brisbane enjoys a mild, subtropical climate. The average daily temperature range is 22 to 30°C in summer and 12 to 22°C in winter. For more information see [tourism information links](https://parks.qld.gov.au/parks/fort-lytton/about#tourism_information_links).

### Fuel and supplies

Fuel and supplies are available in Brisbane. For more information see [tourism information links](https://parks.qld.gov.au/parks/fort-lytton/about#tourism_information_links).

## Our booking system is currently unavailable

We have temporarily deactivated our Queensland National Parks booking system following a cyber security incident involving our third-party booking provider, Bookeasy.

**Queensland's national parks, state forests and recreation areas remain open.**

While the booking system is unavailable:

- **Camping:** You can camp without a camping permit.
- **Vehicle access:** You can access K'gari, Cooloola and Bribie Island recreation areas without a vehicle access permit.
- **Visitor centres:** David Fleay Wildlife Park, Mon Repos Turtle Centre and Walkabout Creek Discovery Centre remain open.
- **Tours and experiences:** Existing bookings, including Mon Repos Turtle Tours, will go ahead. New bookings can be made on the day of the tour, in person, pending capacity.
- **Existing bookings:** All existing bookings and permits remain valid, however we cannot make changes or cancellations.
- **Permits**: Visitors will not be penalised for not holding a camping or vehicle access permit while the temporary arrangements are in place.
- **Future bookings:** Please refer to our website for any updates.

Check [Park Alerts](https://parks.qld.gov.au/park-alerts) for the open/closed status of individual parks before travelling.

For booking enquiries, please email [QPWS@detsi.qld.gov.au](mailto:QPWS@detsi.qld.gov.au).

**All other normal rules apply when visiting our National Parks.**
