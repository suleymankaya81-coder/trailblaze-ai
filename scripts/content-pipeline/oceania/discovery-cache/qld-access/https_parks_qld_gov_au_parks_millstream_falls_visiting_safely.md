## Getting there and getting around

- [Tablelands parks and forests (south) map(PDF, 276KB)](https://parks.qld.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0017/161243/tablelands-south-map.pdf)

Ravenshoe is 147km from Cairns via the Gillies Highway. Big Millstream Falls is 3.5km south-west of Ravenshoe on the Kennedy Highway. Turn left into the signposted entrance and follow the 1km road to the picnic area. Parking is available for long vehicles.

Little Millstream Falls is accessed by a separate route. From Ravenshoe, travel along Tully Falls Road for 3km and then turn right into Wooroora Road. Follow this for 1.5km to the turn-off to the car park.

### Wheelchair accessibility

The toilet at Big Millstream Falls is wheelchair-accessible with assistance.

## Staying safe

- Beware of other road users. Vehicles, cyclists and pedestrians use the roads in this park.
- Obey speed limits and safety and advisory signs.
- Let a responsible person know your travel plans and when you expect to return.
- Always carry water, wear a hat and sturdy footwear, and try to walk in the cooler part of the day.
- Stay on the track and take care on uneven surfaces, especially in wet conditions.

For more information, please read the guidelines on [safety in parks and forests](https://parks.qld.gov.au/before-you-visit/visit-with-care).

## Before you visit

### Essentials to bring

To enjoy your time at Millstream Falls National Park remember to bring:

- sunscreen
- hat
- long-sleeved shirt.

### Opening hours

Millstream Falls National Park is open 24 hours a day. Parts of the park are occasionally closed for seasonal, planned burns. See [park alerts](https://parks.qld.gov.au/park-alerts) for up-to-date information.

### Permits and fees

Permits are required for [commercial](https://www.business.qld.gov.au/industries/hospitality-tourism-sport/tourism/parks-recreation-forests) or [organised events](https://apps.des.qld.gov.au/forms/organised-events).  View [permits and fees](https://parks.qld.gov.au/before-you-visit/permits-and-fees) for further information.

### Pets

Domestic animals are not permitted in Millstream Falls National Park. On-the-spot fines apply.

### Climate and weather

The lower humidity and daytime temperatures at Millstream Falls National Park are a pleasant escape from the coastal extremes. Maximum summer temperatures are around 29°C while winter temperatures can fall below 10°C, when frosts are not uncommon. Most of the rain falls during the wet season, between November and March. For more information, see [the tourism information links](https://parks.qld.gov.au/parks/millstream-falls/about#tourism_information_links).

### Fuel and supplies

Fuel and supplies are available from Ravenshoe. For more information, see the [tourism information links](https://parks.qld.gov.au/parks/millstream-falls/about#tourism_information_links).

## Our booking system is currently unavailable

We have temporarily deactivated our Queensland National Parks booking system following a cyber security incident involving our third-party booking provider, Bookeasy.

**Queensland's national parks, state forests and recreation areas remain open.**

While the booking system is unavailable:

- **Camping:** You can camp without a camping permit.
- **Vehicle access:** You can access K'gari, Cooloola and Bribie Island recreation areas without a vehicle access permit.
- **Visitor centres:** David Fleay Wildlife Park, Mon Repos Turtle Centre and Walkabout Creek Discovery Centre remain open.
- **Tours and experiences:** Existing bookings, including Mon Repos Turtle Tours, will go ahead. New bookings can be made on the day of the tour, in person, pending capacity.
- **Existing bookings:** All existing bookings and permits remain valid, however we cannot make changes or cancellations.
- **Permits**: Visitors will not be penalised for not holding a camping or vehicle access permit while the temporary arrangements are in place.
- **Future bookings:** Please refer to our website for any updates.

Check [Park Alerts](https://parks.qld.gov.au/park-alerts) for the open/closed status of individual parks before travelling.

For booking enquiries, please email [QPWS@detsi.qld.gov.au](mailto:QPWS@detsi.qld.gov.au).

**All other normal rules apply when visiting our National Parks.**
