## Getting there and getting around

Hasties Swamp National Park is 4km south of Atherton on the Atherton–Herberton Road. Turn left into Hastie Road and then right into Koci Road. The bird hide is 1.3km along this well-formed, gravel road.

### Wheelchair accessibility

The toilets and lower floor of the bird hide are wheelchair-accessible.

## Staying safe

- Protect yourself from the sun. Wear sunscreen, a hat, sunglasses and a long-sleeved shirt, even on cloudy days.
- Native bats sometimes roost in the bird hide and leave thin black droppings on hand rails or bench seats. Please wash hands thoroughly if you come into contact with bat droppings, and do not disturb the bats if they are present.
- Take care on the steps of the bird hide, especially in wet weather.

For more information, read the guidelines on [safety in parks and forests](https://parks.qld.gov.au/before-you-visit/visit-with-care).

## Before you visit

### Essentials to bring

To enjoy your time at Hasties Swamp National Park remember to bring:

- drinking water
- binoculars
- bird field guide.

### Opening hours

Hasties Swamp National Park is open 24 hours a day. Parts of the park are occasionally closed for seasonal, planned burns. See [park alerts](https://parks.qld.gov.au/park-alerts) for up-to-date information.

### Permits and fees

Permits are required for [commercial](https://www.business.qld.gov.au/industries/hospitality-tourism-sport/tourism/starting-up/regulations/parks-recreation-forests) or [organised events](https://apps.des.qld.gov.au/forms/organised-events/). View [permits and fees](https://parks.qld.gov.au/before-you-visit/permits-and-fees) for further information.

### Pets

Domestic animals are not permitted in Hasties Swamp National Park.

### Climate and weather

The lower humidity and daytime temperatures at Hasties Swamp National Park are a pleasant escape from the coastal extremes. Maximum summer temperatures for Atherton are around 29°C while winter temperatures can fall below 10°C at night. Most of the rain falls during the wet season, between November and March. For more information, see the [tourism information links](https://parks.qld.gov.au/parks/hasties-swamp/about#tourism_information_links).

### Fuel and supplies

Fuel and supplies are available from Atherton. For more information, see the [tourism information links](https://parks.qld.gov.au/parks/hasties-swamp/about#tourism_information_links).

## Our booking system is currently unavailable

We have temporarily deactivated our Queensland National Parks booking system following a cyber security incident involving our third-party booking provider, Bookeasy.

**Queensland's national parks, state forests and recreation areas remain open.**

While the booking system is unavailable:

- **Camping:** You can camp without a camping permit.
- **Vehicle access:** You can access K'gari, Cooloola and Bribie Island recreation areas without a vehicle access permit.
- **Visitor centres:** David Fleay Wildlife Park, Mon Repos Turtle Centre and Walkabout Creek Discovery Centre remain open.
- **Tours and experiences:** Existing bookings, including Mon Repos Turtle Tours, will go ahead. New bookings can be made on the day of the tour, in person, pending capacity.
- **Existing bookings:** All existing bookings and permits remain valid, however we cannot make changes or cancellations.
- **Permits**: Visitors will not be penalised for not holding a camping or vehicle access permit while the temporary arrangements are in place.
- **Future bookings:** Please refer to our website for any updates.

Check [Park Alerts](https://parks.qld.gov.au/park-alerts) for the open/closed status of individual parks before travelling.

For booking enquiries, please email [QPWS@detsi.qld.gov.au](mailto:QPWS@detsi.qld.gov.au).

**All other normal rules apply when visiting our National Parks.**
