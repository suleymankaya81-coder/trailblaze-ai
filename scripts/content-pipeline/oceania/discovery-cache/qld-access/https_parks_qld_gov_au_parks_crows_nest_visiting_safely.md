## Getting there and getting around

- [Crows Nest National Park map(PDF, 182KB)](https://parks.qld.gov.au/__data/assets/pdf_file/0025/157804/crows-nest-np.pdf)

Turn off the New England Highway at the police station at Crows Nest (44km north of Toowoomba) and take the sealed road 6km to the park.

Caution: after heavy rain, creek crossings may be flooded.

### Wheelchair accessibility

Crows Nest National Park is not wheelchair accessible.

## Staying safe

Your safety is our concern, but your responsibility. Please exercise caution and sound judgement during your visit.

- Do not enter areas designated as restricted access—these areas are closed to ensure your safety.
- Never dive or jump into waterholes. Water may be shallower than it looks or hide submerged objects.
- For your safety, never attempt to climb around the lookout barriers at Crows Nest Falls—to do so could result in serious injury or cost you your life!
- Be careful—rocks and stepping-stones can be slippery, especially after rain.
- Supervise children closely. Some sections of track have steps and steep drop-offs.
- Wear a hat and sturdy walking shoes—avoid bare feet as there could be broken glass.
- Obey all signs.
- Carry drinking water when walking. Treat or boil water supplied at the park before drinking.

### In an emergency

In case of accident or other emergency:

- call Triple Zero (000)
- advise your location and nature of emergency
- stay on the phone until you are told to hang up.

Mobile phone coverage is unreliable.

The nearest hospital is located at Toowoomba.

For more information, please read the guidelines on [safety in parks and forests](https://parks.qld.gov.au/before-you-visit/visit-with-care).

## Before you visit

### Essentials to bring

- Bring sufficient water, food and first-aid supplies.
- Wear sunscreen, a hat and sturdy shoes. Apply insect repellent and carry a raincoat.
- Rubbish bins are not provided. Please bring rubbish bags and take all recyclables and rubbish with you when you leave. Secure supplies and rubbish from goannas and currawongs.
- Preferably use fuel or gas stoves. Gas barbecues are available free of charge in the day-use area. If you are camping and wish to use the wood barbecues provided, please bring your own clean, milled firewood. Never collect wood from the bush. Take care with fire—keep your fire below the grate and make sure your fire is out before you leave it; especially during hot or windy conditions.
- Bring your camera and binoculars for viewing wildlife. A torch, preferably with a red filter to protect animals' eyes, is useful for spotlighting at night.

### Drinking water

Bring your own drinking water. Treat or boil water collected in the park before drinking.

### Opening hours

Crows Nest National Park is open 24 hours a day.

### Permits and fees

To camp in the national park a permit is required and [fees](https://parks.qld.gov.au/camping/bookings) apply. Camping fees must be paid before you camp overnight. A tag with your booking number must be displayed at your camp site.

- Find out more about [camping in Crows Nest National Park](https://parks.qld.gov.au/parks/crows-nest/things-to-do#camping_and_accommodation).
- Book your camp site [online](https://www.qld.gov.au/camping).
- If you cannot book online, see [camping bookings](https://parks.qld.gov.au/camping/bookings) for other options

### Pets

Domestic animals are not permitted in Crows Nest National Park.

### Climate and weather

Expect warm to hot summers and cool to cold winters. Most rain falls during summer months, often as storms. Weather forecasts are available from the [Bureau of Meteorology(external link)](http://www.bom.gov.au/).

### Fuel and supplies

Fuel and supplies are available at Crows Nest, Hampton and Toowoomba.

## Our booking system is currently unavailable

We have temporarily deactivated our Queensland National Parks booking system following a cyber security incident involving our third-party booking provider, Bookeasy.

**Queensland's national parks, state forests and recreation areas remain open.**

While the booking system is unavailable:

- **Camping:** You can camp without a camping permit.
- **Vehicle access:** You can access K'gari, Cooloola and Bribie Island recreation areas without a vehicle access permit.
- **Visitor centres:** David Fleay Wildlife Park, Mon Repos Turtle Centre and Walkabout Creek Discovery Centre remain open.
- **Tours and experiences:** Existing bookings, including Mon Repos Turtle Tours, will go ahead. New bookings can be made on the day of the tour, in person, pending capacity.
- **Existing bookings:** All existing bookings and permits remain valid, however we cannot make changes or cancellations.
- **Permits**: Visitors will not be penalised for not holding a camping or vehicle access permit while the temporary arrangements are in place.
- **Future bookings:** Please refer to our website for any updates.

Check [Park Alerts](https://parks.qld.gov.au/park-alerts) for the open/closed status of individual parks before travelling.

For booking enquiries, please email [QPWS@detsi.qld.gov.au](mailto:QPWS@detsi.qld.gov.au).

**All other normal rules apply when visiting our National Parks.**
