# Forty Mile Scrub National Park \| Tropical North Queensland

## Getting there and getting around

Travel south from Cairns or north from Townsville to Ravenshoe. Continue south-west along the Kennedy Highway to Mount Garnet. Forty Mile Scrub National Park straddles the Kennedy Highway, 65 km south-west of Mount Garnet. The Kennedy Highway is sealed—conventional vehicles can access the park.

The park may also be reached from the south, via Greenvale, or the west, via the Gulf Developmental Road through Georgetown.

### Wheelchair accessibility

The picnic facilities, toilets and walking track are wheelchair accessible.

## Staying safe

Please remember to make safety a priority when visiting this national park.

- Wear sunscreen, a hat, protective clothing and sturdy footwear.
- Always carry water and try to walk in the cooler part of the day.
- Stay on the walking track provided.

For more information, please read the guidelines on [safety in parks and forests](https://parks.qld.gov.au/before-you-visit/visit-with-care).

## Before you visit

Accommodation can be as far as 50km away from the park, so to avoid disappointment it is recommended you pre-book all accommodation.

### Essentials to bring

To ensure an enjoyable visit please bring:

- rubbish bags
- drinking water
- a hat, sunscreen and sunglasses
- sturdy boots and long pants.

### Opening hours

Forty Mile Scrub National Park is open 24 hrs a day, seven days a week. Camping in the park is prohibited.

### Permits and fees

Permits are required for [commercial](https://www.business.qld.gov.au/industries/hospitality-tourism-sport/tourism/parks-recreation-forests) or [organised events](https://apps.des.qld.gov.au/forms/organised-events/). View [permits and fees](https://parks.qld.gov.au/before-you-visit/permits-and-fees) for further information.

### Pets

Please leave your pets at home. Domestic animals are not permitted in Forty Mile Scrub National Park.

### Climate and weather

During the wetter months, from December to April, monsoonal downpours affect the region. Situated at a higher altitude than the surrounding area, Forty Mile Scrub receives more than its share of this rainfall. Between May and November the region receives very little rain. For more information see the [tourism information links](https://parks.qld.gov.au/parks/forty-mile-scrub/about#tourism_information_links).

### Fuel and supplies

Fuel and supplies are available less than an hour away at Discovery Resorts – Undara and also at Mount Surprise and Mount Garnet. For more information see the [tourism information links](https://parks.qld.gov.au/parks/forty-mile-scrub/about#tourism_information_links).

## Our booking system is currently unavailable

We have temporarily deactivated our Queensland National Parks booking system following a cyber security incident involving our third-party booking provider, Bookeasy.

**Queensland's national parks, state forests and recreation areas remain open.**

While the booking system is unavailable:

- **Camping:** You can camp without a camping permit.
- **Vehicle access:** You can access K'gari, Cooloola and Bribie Island recreation areas without a vehicle access permit.
- **Visitor centres:** David Fleay Wildlife Park, Mon Repos Turtle Centre and Walkabout Creek Discovery Centre remain open.
- **Tours and experiences:** Existing bookings, including Mon Repos Turtle Tours, will go ahead. New bookings can be made on the day of the tour, in person, pending capacity.
- **Existing bookings:** All existing bookings and permits remain valid, however we cannot make changes or cancellations.
- **Permits**: Visitors will not be penalised for not holding a camping or vehicle access permit while the temporary arrangements are in place.
- **Future bookings:** Please refer to our website for any updates.

Check [Park Alerts](https://parks.qld.gov.au/park-alerts) for the open/closed status of individual parks before travelling.

For booking enquiries, please email [QPWS@detsi.qld.gov.au](mailto:QPWS@detsi.qld.gov.au).

**All other normal rules apply when visiting our National Parks.**
