# TrailBlaze AI

MINIALIFE V2 — COMPLETE PRODUCT TRANSFORMATION

Transform the existing Minialife project into a premium AI-powered outdoor travel discovery and planning platform.

This is no longer a camping gear planner.

The new product is a destination-first outdoor travel platform focused on:

Camping

Glamping

RV Travel

Campervan Travel

Tiny House Escapes

Cabin Stays

Outdoor Road Trips

Nature Getaways

National Parks

Adventure Travel

The platform should feel like a combination of:

Airbnb

Roadtrippers

Hipcamp

Expedia

Apple

while remaining extremely simple and conversion-focused.

The user should immediately understand:

"Tell us where you want to go and we'll show you where to stay, what to rent, what to do, and what to bring."

DESIGN SYSTEM

Use the visual style and color mood inspired by:

https://home.medvi.org/

Characteristics:

Premium

Soft earth tones

Sage green

Warm beige

Natural backgrounds

Modern cards

Rounded corners

Calm luxury feeling

High-end travel aesthetic

Primary Colors:

Sage Green

Forest Green

Warm Sand

Soft Beige

Off White

Avoid:

Bright blue

Tech startup colors

Neon colors

Dark SaaS dashboards

The site should feel like a luxury outdoor lifestyle brand.

IMAGE STRATEGY

DO NOT use illustrations.

DO NOT use AI generated placeholders.

Use real photography.

Images must come from:

Unsplash

Pexels

Other free high quality photography sources

Use large cinematic photography throughout the site.

Images should include:

National parks

Campgrounds

RV travel

Campervans

Tiny houses

Cabins

Glamping tents

Scenic roads

Lakes

Mountains

Forests

Photography should create emotional desire to travel.

CORE PHILOSOPHY

Do NOT build a complex AI onboarding flow.

Do NOT ask many questions before showing results.

Follow Airbnb and Booking principles.

User enters:

Destination

OR

Trip idea

Examples:

Yellowstone

Yosemite

Colorado

Lake Tahoe

RV Road Trip

Glamping Weekend

Tiny House Escape

Immediately generate destination results.

AI should work behind the scenes.

The user should not feel like they are filling out a form.

DATA ARCHITECTURE

Template-first architecture.

AI-enhanced.

Not AI-generated from scratch.

Flow:

User Search
→ Destination Template
→ Data Aggregation
→ AI Enhancement
→ Output Page

AI should enrich data.

AI should not create fake travel data.

BUSINESS MODEL

Primary Revenue:

Accommodation bookings

RV rentals

Campervan rentals

Activities

Travel insurance

eSIM

Gear rentals

Equipment affiliate products

Focus on bookings and rentals first.

Products are secondary.

USER EXPERIENCE GOAL

Users should feel:

"I found everything I need for this trip in one place."

Not:

"I used an AI tool."

AI must be invisible.

The experience must feel premium, fast, trustworthy and travel-focused.
