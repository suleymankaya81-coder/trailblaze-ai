- Destination page sections live in `src/components/destination/*`; the route file composes them. Why: the single route file grew too large to maintain.
- All Awin links, including bespoke promotional cards, must use buildAwinLink from the shared affiliate module. Why: a single publisher identity prevents attribution drift between cards.
- Homepage rental cards query RVs, campervans, bicycles, cars and gear separately; bundled illustrations match the rental type and AI imagery is explicitly labeled as not actual listings. Why: alphabetical car offers must not crowd out other rental types or imply verified inventory.
- Road trip maps use MapLibre + OpenFreeMap tiles with routes precomputed once by `scripts/content-pipeline/compute-routes.ts` into `src/data/routes.json`; never call a paid/runtime map or routing API per visit. Why: zero per-visitor map cost.
- Destination pages show Awin and informational offers, plus Motorhome Republic (fleet-only) RV rentals and REI gear as explicitly labeled direct non-affiliate links; Awin clicks redirect via `src/lib/affiliates/awin.ts`. Why: Motorhome Republic refuses sites that also list peer-to-peer RV platforms; readers asked for real RV and gear options without implying those brands are Awin partners.
- Search pages live in `seo_landing_pages` (linked to destinations) and are served by the single-segment root route `src/routes/$landing.tsx`; content is generated once by `scripts/content-pipeline/{generate,insert}-landing-pages.py`, never per visit. Why: static routes outrank a dynamic segment, so fixed routes stay unambiguous, and content stays reviewable in the DB.
- Destination photos come from Wikimedia Commons with credits in destinations.photo_credit; a published cover photo is required and must be unique (DB index). Why: stock photos were reused across places and showed the wrong scenery.
- Search pages prefer independently credited covers; when unavailable, use that destination's credited hero photo, never another destination's photo; unverified offer photos are omitted. Why: every guide needs a relevant image without suggesting unverified partner inventory.
- Catalog destinations go live in waves via `scripts/content-pipeline/{prepare-wave,apply-wave}.py` → `generate/insert-landing-pages.py` → `publish-wave.py`; a place is published only when every check passes (cover, location, offers, 4 guides, route, card photos). Why: keeps new places at the same quality as the original 20.
- Destination plans omit packing and extra-information panels; AI enrichment excludes packing and wave preparation collects only activity card photos. Why: avoid unnecessary generation credits and image sourcing for unused content.
- Tier 1 wave preparation saves reusable non-AI photo, coordinate and route facts before paid AI generation; publish only after the existing completeness checks. Why: a credit limit must not waste repeated fact collection or expose incomplete places.
- New search-guide pages must be at least 600 words (generator rewrites once, insert marks shorter pages noindex); earlier pages keep their original review. Why: thin pages rank poorly.
- Bulk guide generation (generate-landing-pages.py) uses google/gemini-3-flash-preview via streamed chat completions. Why: user chose it for lower cost with the same 600+ word checks.
- Search titles are trimmed to ~60 chars and meta descriptions to 160 by insert-landing-pages.py; destination meta descriptions are composed from tagline, best season and guide topics. Why: Google truncates longer snippets.
- Old-design addresses (/plans, /blog, /curated-plans, /returns, …) are answered in `src/lib/seo/legacy.ts` with 410 Gone or a 301 to a clear modern equivalent, never blocked in robots.txt. Why: Google drops 410s faster and must be able to see them.
- `/sitemap.xml` is a sitemap index whose children (`/sitemaps/*.xml`: pages, destinations, one per guide type) are built live in `sitemap.server.ts`. Why: per-file indexing counts in Search Console.
- Travelpayouts brands render as our own link cards (tpm.lv links + sub_id per destination), never iframe widgets; Aviasales fares are cached 24h per airport in flight_price_cache. Why: fast pages and no per-visit API cost.
- Before-road service rows use the shared approved-provider mapping and replace matching catalog rows rather than duplicating them. Why: preparation services stay together and direct visitors to approved tracked partners.
- Service-card photography uses a shared credited manifest and subject-based selector across home, destination, guide and partner cards; place-specific activities retain their own photos. Why: grid position must not substitute a car for a campervan or a landscape for lodging.
- Pinterest RSS uses published/indexable pages and pre-rendered `pin_assets`; `pin_schedule.py` assigns account-wide UTC releases, preserving dates on retries. Cards use private storage, signed URLs, shared pinCopy and image credits. Why: no per-visit rendering, duplicate rescheduling or quota multiplication across boards.
